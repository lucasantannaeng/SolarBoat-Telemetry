# 🛡️ Technical Specification: Anti-Sloshing Baffles

## 📌 Overview
**Baffles** (longitudinal and transversal deflector plates) are global hydrodynamic damping elements installed inside the vessel's bilge compartment. They dissipate fluid kinetic energy during pitch, roll, and surge motions, mitigating sloshing resonance and preserving vessel stability.

---

## 📐 Geometric Parameters

- **Material:** Structural Rigid PVC Sheets
- **Dimensions per Baffle Plate:** $1000\text{ mm} \times 200\text{ mm} \times 4\text{ mm}$
- **Total Volume of Baffle System:** $0.00158865\text{ m}^3$
- **Equalization Perforations:**
  - **Hole Diameter:** $10\text{ mm}$
  - **Perforation Array:** Matrix grid of holes designed to allow slow static water level equalisation while preventing dynamic wave propagation.

---

## ⚖️ Physical Properties

- **Material Density ($\rho$):** $1400\text{ kg/m}^3$
- **Total Mass of Baffles ($m$):** $2.22411\text{ kg}$
- **Apparent Weight:** $2.22411\text{ kgf}$

---

## ⚙️ Hydrodynamic & Naval Architecture Impact
1. **Free Surface Effect (FSE) Reduction:** Prevents drastic reduction of metacentric height ($\overline{GM}$), mitigating capsizing risks during sharp cornering maneuvers in competition.
2. **Kinetic Energy Dissipation:** Converts turbulent fluid kinetic energy into head loss as water flows through the perforation matrix.
3. **Electrical Infrastructure Protection:** Prevents heavy water splashing onto solar motor controllers and battery banks.
