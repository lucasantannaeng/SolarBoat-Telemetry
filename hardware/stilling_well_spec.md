# 🌊 Technical Specification: Stilling-Well (Calm Well)

## 📌 Overview
The **Stilling-Well** (calm well chamber) serves as the primary local hydraulic mechanical filter. It dampens high-frequency surface perturbations, waves, and spray generated by vessel dynamics (*sloshing*), providing a smooth, stationary water surface for ultrasonic distance measurement.

---

## 📐 Geometric & Hydraulic Parameters

- **Material:** Rigid Schedule PVC Pipe
- **Nominal Outer Diameter ($D$):** $100\text{ mm}$ ($0.10\text{ m}$)
- **Total Height ($H$):** $30\text{ cm}$ ($0.30\text{ m}$)
- **Wall Thickness ($e$):** $2.5\text{ mm}$
- **Bottom Equalization Holes (Hydraulic Communication):**
  - **Hole Diameter ($d_f$):** $6\text{ mm}$ to $8\text{ mm}$
  - **Hole Count:** 4 radial holes spaced $90^\circ$ apart, located $2\text{ cm}$ above the base.
  - **Total Inlet Area:** $\approx 1.13\text{ cm}^2$ to $2.01\text{ cm}^2$

---

## ⚖️ Physical Properties

- **PVC Material Volume:** $0.0000873\text{ m}^3$
- **PVC Density ($\rho$):** $1400\text{ kg/m}^3$
- **Calculated Mass ($m$):** $0.12222\text{ kg}$ ($122.22\text{ g}$)
- **Apparent Weight:** $0.12222\text{ kgf}$

---

## ⚙️ Function in System Architecture
1. **Quiescent Water Surface:** Dampens surface turbulence and air bubbles to ensure clean acoustic echoes.
2. **Acoustic Waveguide:** Directs the $40\text{ kHz}$ ultrasonic pulse beam perpendicular to the water surface, preventing multi-path reflections off internal hull ribs.
3. **Flow Restriction Filter:** The restricted inlet hole area acts as a mechanical low-pass filter, smoothing rapid water level surges.
